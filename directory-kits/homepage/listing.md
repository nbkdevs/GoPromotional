### Description (220 words; target 150-300)

GoPromotional is a UK supplier of custom-printed promotional products and promotional gifts, founded in 2010 by Gareth Parkin. The head office is near Sheffield in South Yorkshire, with further offices in London and Dublin.

The website brings together more than 50,000 products that can be personalised with a company logo, grouped into clear categories: pens and pencils, notes and paper products, bags and backpacks, travel mugs, technology and gadgets, wearables, outdoor and leisure, lifestyle, notebooks, drinkware, toys and novelties, and trade show and event items. Each product page shows prices and minimum quantities, and a fast quote form sits next to every item.

The service behind the site is built around people rather than a self-service checkout. A dedicated account manager helps buyers compare products and deadlines, the in-house artwork team checks every logo and sends a PDF proof, and production only starts once the proof is approved. Branding methods include screen printing, UltraHD full colour printing, laser engraving, embroidery, foil printing and embossing.

Other features include free samples on most items, a price match promise on like-for-like quotes, over 3,500 products with environmental credentials, a UK-made range, and express 48 or 72-hour delivery on selected lines. For anyone looking for branded merchandise for events, staff or clients, the homepage is a good starting point for exploring the full range.

## How ordering works

GoPromotional is not a DIY ordering tool. Every enquiry goes to a dedicated account manager who compares products, quantities, branding methods, prices and deadlines with you:

1. **Choose**: explain what the campaign needs to achieve and get recommendations.
2. **Artwork**: the in-house studio checks your logo and emails a PDF proof.
3. **Approve**: nothing is printed until you sign off the final layout.
4. **Deliver**: most items are branded within 5-7 working days, with 48 or 72-hour express options from the London warehouse.
