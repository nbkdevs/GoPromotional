Pack Quick privacy policy

Applies to the Pack Quick browser extension for Firefox, Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome. Effective 19 October 2026.

Summary: Pack Quick does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays in your browser’s local extension storage on your own device.

What Pack Quick does

Manage packing lists in your toolbar: multiple lists, templates, filters and copy-to-clipboard.

Data stored on your device

To remember your entries between popup opens, Pack Quick saves the following using the browser’s extension storage API (storage.local):

  • the names of your packing lists
  • the items on each list and whether each item is ticked as packed
  • which list was open last

This data is stored only in your browser profile on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not synchronised to any account or cloud service by the extension.

Data we collect

None. The extension has no server, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, crash reporting or remotely hosted code. We do not receive your browsing history, page content, location, contacts, identifiers or any other information.

Permissions

PermissionWhy it is needed
storageSaves the data listed above locally so it is still there the next time you open the popup.

Pack Quick requests no host permissions, no access to tabs or browsing history, and no clipboard permission. The Copy buttons write a short text summary to your clipboard only when you click them.

External link

The popup shows a small footer link, “More: branded tote bags for event marketing”. Nothing happens unless you click it. If you do, the page opens in a new tab and is governed by that website’s own privacy and cookie policies, not this one. The extension does not pass any of your data to that site.

Deleting your data

Use Clear packed or Delete list in the popup to remove items or whole lists. Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored data from your browser.

Children

The extension does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
