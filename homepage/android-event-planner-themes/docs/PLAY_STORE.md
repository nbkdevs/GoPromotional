## Short description (≤80 characters)

Offline event planner with checklists, themes, budgets, and merchandise lists.

## Full description

Event Planner & Themes helps you organise gatherings without an account or internet connection.

Plan conferences, parties, launches, and community events in one place:

• Events — name, type, date, time, venue, attendees, and notes  
• Checklist — priorities, categories, due dates, and a starter planning list  
• Themes — offline ideas for décor, invitations, signage, activities, and merchandise  
• Budget — estimated vs actual spending and remaining balance  
• Merchandise — quantities, unit costs, totals, and order status  

Everything stays on your device. No login. No cloud account required.
