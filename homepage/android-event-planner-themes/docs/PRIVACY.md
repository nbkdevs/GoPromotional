# Privacy Policy — Event Planner & Themes

**Last updated:** 7 October 2026

Event Planner & Themes (“the app”) is an offline event-planning utility.

## What we collect

The app does **not** collect personal data for transmission to a server operated by the developer.

- No account registration
- No cloud sync
- No required analytics or advertising SDKs
- No location tracking

## Where your data is stored

Events, tasks, expenses, merchandise lists, theme favorites, and preferences are stored **locally on your device** using the Android filesystem (Room database and DataStore).

If you uninstall the app, this local data is removed with the app (unless your device backup settings restore it).

## Optional external links

The Merchandise section may include an optional link to an external website for merchandise ideas. Opening that link uses your device browser. The app does not send your planning data with that link.

## Permissions

The app does not require Internet, Camera, Contacts, Location, or Storage permissions for core functionality.

## Contact

For privacy questions about this open project, open an issue in the project repository.
